How to play

Topple the Crimson keep before it topples yours. One shot per turn.

1
Drag back to aim

Pull back from your cannon to set the angle and power. Release to fire.

2
Read the wind

Wind pushes every shot. The dotted arc shows your predicted path.

3
Tilt for fine aim

Optional. Tap Tilt aim and tilt your device. On iPhone and iPad, iOS asks for motion permission the first time you enable it.

Keyboard: ↑ ↓ angle · ← → power · Space fires
